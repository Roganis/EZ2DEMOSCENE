1Welcome

EZ2DEMOSCENE is a tool for making short, perfectly looping 3D animations in the style of the demoscene: glowing neon arenas, mirrored golden halls, glossy crystals orbited by debris, raymarched tunnels, kaleidoscopes and synthwave sunsets. The loops are for music visuals, VJ sets, wallpapers, social posts and GIFs.

You never write code or shaders. You pick a starting scene, then change it with sliders, colour pickers and menus. When you like it, you export an MP4, WebM, GIF or a folder of PNG frames.

The one big idea: everything loops

A scene is exactly a whole number of beats long, at a tempo you choose (BPM). Every motion in EZ2DEMOSCENE completes a whole number of times during that loop. That includes spins, orbits, pulses, particles, scrolling textures, drifting clouds and even film grain. So the last frame leads straight back into the first, with no jump.

Why this mattersYou never have to "fix the loop" by hand. Whatever you change, the export repeats seamlessly. This is why many settings ask for a count of turns per loop or cycles per loop rather than a speed.

How a scene is built

A scene is a stack of layers drawn together:

  • Background: the sky or environment behind everything (nebula, tunnel, plasma…).
  • Mirror floor: a reflective ground plane, optionally with a glowing grid.
  • Shapes: 3D objects, possibly copied many times in rings, grids, walls or swarms.
  • Particles: sparks, stars, glitter, vortices.

On top of the layers sit the camera, the light & fog, and the post effects that give the final look (glow, kaleidoscope, retro palettes, CRT…).

2Installing & launching

What you need

ItemDetails
A graphics cardAny GPU with Vulkan, DirectX 12 or Metal support (most GPUs from 2015 onward). A software renderer also works, only slower.
Rust 1.95 or newerNeeded to build the app from source. Install it from rustup.rs, or update with rustup update stable.
ffmpeg (optional)Needed only for MP4, WebM and GIF export. Download it from ffmpeg.org and make sure the ffmpeg command works in a terminal. PNG sequences need nothing extra.
Linux onlysudo apt install libasound2-dev libxkbcommon-dev libwayland-dev

Building and starting

git clone https://github.com/Roganis/EZ2DEMOSCENE
cd EZ2DEMOSCENE
cargo run --release                   # opens the editor
cargo run --release -- my.ez2.json    # opens a saved project

The first build downloads and compiles libraries, so it takes a few minutes. Later starts are instant. The finished program is target/release/ez2demoscene (ez2demoscene.exe on Windows), and you can copy it anywhere.

When the editor opens you are greeted by the preset gallery.

4Quick start: your first loop in five minutes

  1. Pick a preset. In the gallery, click Orbiting Solid. The scene starts playing straight away.
  2. Change a colour. Click the Dodecahedron layer on the left. In the inspector under Material, click the Colour swatch and pick a hot pink.
  3. Make it glow on the beat. Still in Material, find Glow and click the ~ button next to it. Set the wave to Pulse, amount to 3 and ×/loop to 8 (the loop is 8 beats long). It now flashes on every beat.
  4. Add particles. Click + Add → ✨ Particles → Vortex. In the new layer, set Colour (young) to white and Colour (old) to pink.
  5. Add a retro touch. Select Post effects on the left and tick Retro palette. Choose PICO-8.
  6. Export. Press Ctrl+E. Pick Animated GIF (the size switches to 480×270, a good size for GIFs), choose a file with Browse… and press ⏺ Export.
  7. Save your project with Ctrl+S to keep editing it later.
Made a mess?Ctrl+Z undoes any change, including randomizing and loading presets.

5Layers

The layer list works like the layers in a paint program. Each row has a visibility tick box and the layer's name. Click a row to edit it. Under the list, the buttons ⬆ ⬇ ⧉ 🗑 move, duplicate or delete the selected layer, and right-clicking a layer shows the same options.

5.1 Backgrounds

A background fills the whole screen behind the other layers and follows the camera. Every background has three colours (Colour A is usually the darkest), a Motion / loop count, a Brightness and a Detail (pattern scale).

StyleDescription
Gradient skyA soft two-colour sky with a horizon glow and a sun halo in the light direction.
Nebula cloudsSwirling fractal clouds with stars. The clouds drift on a closed path so they loop.
StarfieldTwinkling stars over faint coloured dust.
Raymarched tunnelThe classic demo tunnel flying forward through a winding tube. Pick a Wall texture (the XOR or checker textures are great).
Raymarched fractalA volumetric "Kaliset" fractal space. It looks psychedelic and cosmic, and is heavy on slow GPUs.
Oldschool plasmaThe 90s sine plasma.
Synthwave sun & gridA striped sun sinking into a scrolling neon grid, with stars.
TipYou can stack two backgrounds, but the later one covers the earlier one. Usually one is enough.

5.2 Mirror floor

A large reflective ground plane. The scene is really reflected in it, including particles and the sky.

  • Reflection: 0 is matte, 1 is a perfect mirror. Blur gives a frosted, polished-stone look. Reflection tint colours the reflection (gold for a gold room).
  • Texture and Tiling put a pattern on the floor. The led_grid texture makes an LED dance floor.
  • Neon grid draws glowing lines. Set Grid glow, Grid colour, Grid scale and Scroll / loop (cells per loop) for the synthwave "driving" effect.
  • Floor height (in Placement & motion) moves the floor up or down.
One mirrorOnly the first mirror floor in the list reflects. Extra ones are ignored.

5.3 Shapes

In Shape choose one of 14 built-in shapes, or a 3D file:

ShapeGood for
Cube, Tetrahedron, Octahedron, Dodecahedron, IcosahedronClassic demo solids. Try them with Faceted on and Glow where: Edges.
Sphere (Detail 0–5)Orbs and bubbles. Detail 0 gives a faceted low-poly ball.
TorusRings and chain links (Thickness, Segments).
Cylinder (Sides)Platforms and pillars. Eight sides makes an octagonal stage.
Shard, Crystal clusterSharp spikes and crystal mountains. Seed gives a different random shape.
Panel (Bevel)Sci-fi plates, light panels, screens.
Ring band (Arc, Width, Height)Neon rings and arcs. Set Arc below 360° for partial arcs and chevrons.
Plane, PyramidSimple building blocks.
3D model fileYour own .gltf, .glb or .obj. It is centred and scaled to fit automatically, and uses the layer's material.

Copies (instancing)

One layer can show many copies of its shape. Pick a Layout:

LayoutSettingsExample
Singlen/aOne hero object.
GridCount x/y/z, SpacingSpeaker stacks, cube cities.
RadialCount, Radius. Copies face outward.Panels around an arena, crystal skyline.
ScatterCount, Radius, Only surface, SeedFloating debris, asteroid shells.
Orbit swarmCount, Radius, Spread, Orbits / loop, SeedDebris orbiting a planet.
WallColumns, Rows, Spacing, CurveOrb walls, LED walls. Curve bends the wall into an arc.
SpiralCount, Radius, Height, TurnsChandeliers, DNA helices.

Variation (appears when there are copies) adds life:

  • Random tilt, Random size, Random hue and Random spin make every copy a little different. Change Seed to reshuffle them.
  • Size wave is a wave of growing and shrinking that travels across the copies.
  • Light chase is a bright glow running along the copies, like marquee lights.
  • Waves / loop sets how many times those waves pass per loop, and Wavelengths sets how many waves fit across all copies.

Placement & motion

SettingMeaning
Position, RotationWhere the layer sits and how it is turned (degrees).
SizeThe size of the object, or of each copy. It does not change the distances between copies.
StretchStretch per axis (make a cube into a slab or a pillar).
Spin / loopWhole turns per loop around x, y and z. Negative numbers spin the other way.
BobUp/down offset. Animate it with ~ to make objects float.

5.4 Symmetry

Symmetry copies the whole layer around the centre of the world:

  • Mirror X (left/right), Mirror Z (front/back), Mirror XZ (four copies).
  • Radial: N rotated copies around the vertical axis.
  • Kaleido: N copies where every other one is mirrored, giving a kaleidoscope pattern.
TipBuild one side of a room, then add Mirror X. The Gold Kaleido Room's orb walls, speakers and light panels are each built once and mirrored.

5.5 Particles

EmitterMotion
BurstExplodes outward from the centre.
Sphere driftFloats gently inside a sphere.
Ring orbitCircles in a flat ring (Speed = whole orbits per loop).
FountainShoots up and falls back.
Warp starsStreams towards the viewer (hyperspace). Add a Trail for streaks.
VortexSpirals upwards like embers.
Snow / glitterFalls slowly and sparkles.

Count is the number of particles. Lives / loop is how many times each particle is reborn in one loop, so higher means faster. Area is the size of the emitter. The look is controlled by Sprite (soft glow, pixel square, star, ring), Size, two colours (young → old), Brightness and Trail (ghost copies behind each particle).

PerformanceParticles are cheap. Tens of thousands are fine on a normal GPU. Trails multiply the count, so count × (trail + 1) is what gets drawn.

6Materials, glow & textures

Surface

SettingEffect
ColourThe base colour.
Metallic0 looks like plastic or paint. 1 looks like metal, where reflections take on the colour (gold, chrome, copper).
Roughness0 is a glossy mirror-like finish, 1 is matte. The glossy black look of Neon Arena is black + metallic 0.9 + roughness 0.15.
FacetedFlat-shaded, low-poly look. Every face catches light separately.
Rim lightA glow along the silhouette that separates objects from dark backgrounds.

Glow (emission)

Glow colour and Glow (strength) make a surface emit light. Bright glow feeds the bloom post effect and produces the neon halo. Glow where decides which part glows:

Glow whereResult
FullThe whole surface glows (neon tubes, light panels).
EdgesOnly the outlines of each face glow, for a Tron / wireframe-neon look. Works on the solids, panels, cylinders, shards and pyramids.
StripesGlowing horizontal stripes.
TextureBright parts of the texture glow (LED grids, circuits, tech-panel slits).

Hue shift rotates all the colours of the layer. Animate it with a Saw wave for rainbow cycling.

Your own images & "retro-ize"

Import images (PNG, JPG, BMP, GIF, TGA) in any of these ways: from a texture menu (Import image…), from File → Import image…, or by dragging them onto the window. A dropped image is applied to the selected shape. All your images are listed under Your images, where you can tick Retro-ize. This shrinks the image to Max size pixels and remaps it to a classic Palette with Dithering, turning any photo into a 90s texture.

7Animating anything

Every value with a ~ button next to it can move over time. The button turns pink when the value is animated. Click it to open the animation panel:

ControlMeaning
WaveSine (smooth), Triangle (linear back and forth), Saw (ramp then jump), Square (on/off), Pulse (sharp hit that fades, the beat flash), Random (a new random value each cycle).
amountHow far the value swings around the slider value. 0 means not animated.
×/loopWhole cycles per loop. Use the number of beats in the loop to hit every beat, half of it for every other beat, and so on. Negative numbers run backwards.
offsetShifts the wave in time (0–1), to stagger several animations.
♪Adds the music's loudness (see chapter 10).

Recipes

  • Pulse on every beat: Glow → Pulse, amount 3, ×/loop = loop beats (e.g. 16).
  • Breathing camera: Camera Distance → Sine, amount 2, ×/loop 1.
  • Kick zoom: Kaleidoscope Zoom → Pulse, amount 0.2, ×/loop 16.
  • Rainbow: Hue shift → Saw, amount 0.5, ×/loop 1.
  • Floating object: Bob → Sine, amount 0.5, ×/loop 2.
  • Strobe: Glow → Square, ×/loop 32.

Look atThe point the camera aims at.
Distance, HeightHow far away and how high the camera is. Both are animatable.

Light & fog

  • Fog colour / Fog density: distant things fade into this colour. The fog colour is also the "empty space" colour, so match it to your background.
  • Sky light / Ground light / Ambient: soft light from above and below. The sky colour is also what glossy surfaces reflect, which gives black gloss its highlights.
  • Sun colour, Sun strength, Sun direction: the main light.

9Post effects

EffectWhat it does
KaleidoscopeFolds the whole picture into mirrored wedges. You can set Segments, Angle, Turns per loop, Zoom (animatable) and Centre.
Mirror splitMirrors one half of the picture onto the other (left → right, top → bottom, or quad). This is instant symmetry.
Bloom / glowBright things spill light. Intensity sets the strength, Threshold how bright something must be to glow, and Spread how wide the halo is.
Chromatic aberrationAn RGB colour split towards the edges, like a cheap lens. Pulse it on the beat.
PixelateFat pixels (the size is measured at 1080p, so previews match exports).
Retro paletteReduces the image to a classic computer palette with ordered (Bayer) dithering.
CRT monitorScanlines, screen curvature and a VHS wobble.
Colour gradingExposure, Contrast, Saturation, Vignette (dark corners), Film grain and Beat flash (a white flash on every beat).

10Timing & music

Tempo and loop length

Tempo (BPM) and Loop length (in beats) set how long the loop is. For example, 128 BPM × 16 beats = 7.5 seconds. The quick buttons set 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64 beats. These values also appear in the timeline.

Because every animation is counted per loop, changing the BPM speeds the whole scene up or slows it down, and it stays in sync.

Adding music

Use Load music… or drop an MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A or AAC file onto the window. Then:

  • The music plays with the loop and restarts at the loop point. Scrubbing the timeline seeks the music.
  • Its loudness is drawn in the timeline, and brighter bars mean more bass.
  • Any animated value can follow the music with the ♪ amount in its ~ panel.
  • Detect tempo from length sets the BPM so the whole track fits the loop length. This is useful for music written as a loop.
  • MP4 and WebM exports include the music (you can turn this off in the export dialog).
Tip for musiciansSet the BPM to your track's tempo and the loop length to its bar count × 4. The visuals then land exactly on your beats.

11Randomize, Surprise me & undo

  • 🎲 Randomize shuffles the current scene: one harmonious hue rotation of all colours, new shapes and layouts, new motion and a few post-effect changes. Every click gives a new variation.
  • ⚙ opens the Randomizer settings. Choose what may change (Colours, Shapes & layouts, Motion, Post effects), how strongly, and the seed. The same seed always gives the same result.
  • Surprise me picks a random preset and randomizes it strongly.
  • Undo / redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z) covers everything, so randomize freely.

12Nodes mode (advanced)

Click Nodes in the top bar to build the scene as a graph. Layers become source nodes that flow through modifiers into an Output. Everything connected to Output is rendered.

NodeWhat it does
SourceOne layer. Click edit in inspector to edit it on the right with the usual controls.
SymmetryReplaces the symmetry of every incoming layer.
OffsetMoves every incoming layer.
SpinAdds turns per loop.
ScaleMultiplies the size.
TintRotates hues and scales the glow.
ArrayRepeats the incoming layers N times, stepping the position and rotation for each copy.
MergeCombines up to 4 streams.
OutputUp to 8 inputs. Everything here is drawn.

Working with the graph: right-click empty space to add nodes, drag from an output dot to an input dot to connect, and right-click a node to Duplicate or Delete it. The toolbar has three controls: render the graph switches between the graph and the plain layer list; Rebuild from layers starts a new graph from your layers; and Bake to layers turns the graph's result into ordinary layers and returns to Simple mode.

13Exporting your loop

OptionNotes
FormatMP4 (H.264) works everywhere. WebM (VP9) is for the web. Animated GIF is for chats and forums. PNG sequence writes one PNG per frame into a folder and needs no ffmpeg; it is ideal for video editors and VJ software.
SizeType a size or use Presets: 720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K, Square, Vertical (phone), GIF small, Retro 4:3.
Frame rate24, 25, 30, 50 or 60 fps. The dialog shows how many frames one loop needs.
Repeat loopMakes the file longer by repeating the loop. Useful for platforms that don't loop videos.
MusicInclude the music track (MP4/WebM only).
File / FolderWhere to save. Use Browse….

Press ⏺ Export. A progress bar shows the frames being rendered, and you can keep working or press Cancel. Export renders every frame at full quality (anti-aliasing and full-resolution effects) even if your preview is set lower.

GIF tipsGIFs get big quickly. Use 480×270 at 25–30 fps, keep loops short (8 beats), and avoid film grain, which makes GIFs much larger.
"ffmpeg not found"?Install ffmpeg, then either make sure ffmpeg runs in a terminal or paste the path to the ffmpeg program into the box in the export dialog and press Check.

Stills

File → Save still image… saves the current frame as a PNG at the export size.

Command line

You can render without opening the window, which is handy for batch jobs:

ez2demoscene --list-presets
ez2demoscene --render "Neon Arena" still.png --phase 0.25 --size 1920x1080
ez2demoscene --export "Gold Kaleido Room" loop.mp4 --size 1920x1080 --fps 60 --repeats 4
ez2demoscene --export my.ez2.json frames/          # PNG sequence into a folder
ez2demoscene --write-presets my_presets/           # presets as editable project files
ez2demoscene --write-textures my_textures/         # the retro texture pack as PNGs

--phase is a position in the loop from 0 to 1 (0.5 is the middle).

14Recipes

Neon arena (black gloss and red neon)

  1. Background Nebula clouds, with very dark colours A/B and a red C. Set the fog colour to almost-black red.
  2. Add a Mirror floor: colour black, Reflection 0.35, Blur 0.3.
  3. Add Crystal cluster with Copies → Radial (count 50, radius 34), Size 7, Random tilt 18, Random size 0.5. Use a glossy black material (metallic 0.9, roughness 0.15, Faceted).
  4. Add Panel with Radial (20, radius 11) and Stretch 2.6 / 0.35 / 1.2. Glow red, Edges, strength 5, and Light chase 1.
  5. Add Ring band (Width 0.02) stretched 16 / 1 / 16 with red glow, Full, and a Sine animation.
  6. Add Vortex particles in orange → red. Turn on Bloom 1.1 and Chromatic aberration.

Golden mirrored room

  1. Background Gradient sky in warm golds. Set the light to warm white and the ambient to 0.45.
  2. Add a Mirror floor with Reflection 0.85, Reflection tint gold, texture led_grid and Grid glow 0.6.
  3. Add Sphere with Copies → Wall (14 × 5, curve 50°), Size 0.33, Rotation y 90, Position x 6.5, Symmetry Mirror X, and Light chase 0.6.
  4. Add Cube with texture speaker, a Grid of 2 × 3 and Symmetry Mirror X. Pulse the glow on the beat.
  5. Add Octahedron with Spiral copies high above the floor as a chandelier, plus Snow / glitter star particles.
  6. Post: Mirror split left → right and Bloom 1.0.

Crystal orbited by debris

  1. Background Starfield.
  2. Add Dodecahedron, Size 1.6, blue, metallic 0.5, roughness 0.18, Faceted, Rim light 0.9, Spin / loop x 1, y 1.
  3. Add Cube with Copies → Orbit swarm (70, radius 3.2, spread 0.9), Stretch 0.12 / 0.12 / 0.5 and Random size 0.6.
  4. Add Ring band arcs (Arc 250°) with blue glow and Spin / loop y 2, plus Ring orbit particles with trails.

90s demo tunnel

  1. Background Raymarched tunnel with Wall texture xor and Motion / loop 4.
  2. Add Icosahedron with texture checker, Chunky pixels, Glow Edges, and Spin / loop 2 / 1 / 0.
  3. Add Warp stars with the Pixel square sprite and Trail 4.
  4. Post: Pixelate 3, Retro palette EGA with dithering 0.7, CRT monitor, and Beat flash 0.15.

Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
Playback stuttersLower the preview quality (the % menu above the viewport). The fractal background and big particle counts are the heaviest. Exports are not affected.
Everything is washed out or one colourA big glowing object is probably filling the screen. Reduce Glow, raise the Bloom threshold, or check the Size/Stretch of ring and panel layers.
A shape is invisibleCheck its visibility box, its Size, whether it is below the floor or inside another object, and the fog density.
My model shows as a cubeThe file couldn't be read. The reason appears in red at the bottom of the inspector. Use Edit → Reload models & images after fixing the file.
No soundNo audio output device was found. The music is still used for sync, ♪ modulation and export.
Video export failsCheck ffmpeg (chapter 13), or export a PNG sequence instead.
The app doesn't startUpdate your graphics drivers. On Linux, install Vulkan drivers (e.g. mesa-vulkan-drivers).

Project files

Projects are saved as readable .ez2.json text files. Imported models, images and music are stored as file paths, so keep those files next to your project when moving it to another computer. The built-in presets are also available as project files in assets/presets.

Glossary

TermMeaning
Beat / BPMA musical pulse. BPM is beats per minute.
Loop / phaseThe repeating clip. Phase is a position in it, from 0 (start) to 1 (end, which equals the start).
InstancingDrawing many copies of one shape efficiently.
Emission / glowLight a surface gives off by itself.
BloomThe halo that bright things spill onto their surroundings.
RaymarchingA technique that draws shapes defined by maths (tunnels, fractals) instead of polygons. It is a demoscene classic.
DitheringPatterns of pixels that fake more colours than a palette has.
DemosceneA computer art subculture, dating back to the 1980s, that makes real-time audiovisual "demos".
